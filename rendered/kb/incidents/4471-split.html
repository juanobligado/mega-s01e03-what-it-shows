#4471 is marked known: one issue, one fix queued. Its own data says otherwise. The reports merged into it come from three different problems, and the queued parser fix closes only one of them.

Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships. Two of its problems need fixes of their own.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the fix that is queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. The date is swapped at entry, before the parser sees it, so the parser fix leaves these broken. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 land here.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, which a day/month swap cannot do. The invite shows Monday instead of Tuesday, one client sees a different day from another in a different time zone, one invite arrived before its booking. That points at time-zone handling, not the parser. The desk already noted these reports; the table on #4471 only footnoted them.

The table on #4471 counts the same reports, but it keeps one-day moves inside parser and marks hint as “no ticket of its own”. The intake tags do not help either: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, so en-US browsers get tagged hint wherever the report came from. The cause depends on the tenant and the way in (#lib/kb/causes.ts), not on the browser.

Merging by subject line (decision 0001) is not what merged these: the subjects vary. They were merged because they all looked like “wrong date”.

Lessons

  • A count is not a cause. Before you mark a big ticket known, sort it by cause and read the reports that do not fit.
  • A footnote under a row is a problem without a ticket. Give it one.
